Engineered for Smart Transportation

Built from the ground up for high-efficiency transit operations.

Segment-Based Booking

Maximize bus occupancy. A seat from Panaji to Pernem can be re-booked from Pernem to Ratnagiri on the exact same trip.

Dual QR Ecosystem

Physical seat QR codes initiate instantaneous in-bus bookings, while cryptographically signed digital ticket QR codes enable rapid conductor verification.

Smartphone GPS Tracking

No expensive proprietary hardware needed. Driver smartphones stream live coordinates over WebSockets with automatic stale-detection.

Conductor & No-Show Engine

Real-time passenger manifest with boarding verification and configurable deadline thresholds to confirm no-shows and release seats.

Nearby Bus Stand Finder

Locate nearest registered bus stands and terminals using geolocation with real-time distance calculations and directions.

Fleet Management Radar

Operators monitor their entire authorized fleet on an interactive map radar, viewing active trips, speeds, and driver assignments.
